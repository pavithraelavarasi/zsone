Loops: Try These, Then Come Talk

Eight problems, roughly in order of difficulty. Pick one, actually write and run it yourself, then bring your solution back for review — what matters isn't just whether it works, but why you built it the way you did.

1 Sum of Numbers 1 to N
Warm-upfor looprange()

Inputn = 6
Output21

Input"programming"
Output3

Input47382

7 Live Grade Reporter
Corefor loopif / elif / elserange()

Ask the user for n test scores, one at a time. For each score, print its letter grade immediately — A for 90+, B for 80-89, C for 70-79, F below 70 — without storing the scores anywhere. Process each one as it comes in, then move to the next.

Inputn = 3 scores entered one at a time: 95, 82, 61
OutputA B F
8 First Repeated Digit
Stretchfor loopif conditionstrings

Input4927492
Output4
Before you come for review: know what your loop variable represents at each step, be ready to trace your own code by hand on a new input, and have one edge case in mind (n = 0, a single-digit number, a tie) that you checked or at least thought about. That's usually a better use of the conversation than just confirming the output matched.
